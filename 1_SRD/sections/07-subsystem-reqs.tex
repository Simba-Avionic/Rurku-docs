\section{Department requirements}

\guidance{One section per department. Keep requirements measurable.
Interfaces and diagrams $\rightarrow$ SAD; procedures $\rightarrow$ CONOPS /
Test Plan.}

\subsection{Engine}

\guidance{Engine department owns the propulsion system: chamber, injector,
ignition, and typically onboard tanks / feed hardware that delivers
propellant to the engine. Coordinate remotely actuated valves with GSE.}

\begin{reqtabular}
ENG-001 & Propellant combination shall be \todo{fuel / oxidizer}
  (change requires SRD approval). & Must & I & \todo{} \\
ENG-002 & Engine shall deliver thrust / burn time of
  \todo{T\,N for t\,s} (or performance envelope). & Must & T & \todo{} \\
ENG-003 & Ignition reliability shall be demonstrated in static fire
  per CONOPS success criteria. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
ENG-004 & Onboard feed / tanks shall support cold-flow and flight fueling
  without leaks at operating pressure \todo{P}. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
ENG-005 & MEOP / proof / burst factors for engine-side pressure hardware
  shall meet \todo{team safety standard / code}. & Must & A/T & \todo{} \\
ENG-006 & Engine-side valves required for hot/fueled ops shall be remotely
  operable via GSE. & Must & D/T & \todo{} \\
ENG-007 & \todo{Cutoff / abort / residual dump capability if any.} & \todo{} & \todo{} & \todo{} \\
\end{reqtabular}

\subsection{Avionics}

\begin{reqtabular}
AV-001 & All onboard computers shall have synchronized time. & Must & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-002 & The rocket shall synchronize time with GSE. & Should & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-003 & All actuators shall be secured and shall require at least two
  actions to activate. & Must & I/D & \todo{} \\
AV-004 & All sensor data shall be saved on every computer. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
AV-005 & The rocket shall know its position. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
AV-006 & The rocket shall detect liftoff and allow abort after a failed
  liftoff. & Must & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-007 & The rocket shall be able to auto-tank its tanks. & Could & T & \todo{} \\
AV-008 & The rocket shall be able to reduce tank pressure when required. & Must & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-009 & GSE shall be able to disable AV-008 when personnel need to
  approach the rocket. & Must & D/T & \todo{} \\
AV-010 & All manual actions from GSE (e.g.\ venting) shall take effect
  immediately on the rocket and shall immediately report the change as an
  event. & Must & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-011 & The rocket shall remain on the launch pad for at least 6\,h
  without charging. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
AV-012 & The rocket shall have long-range communication with GSE
  (downlink-only allowed) that works to at least $2\times$ target
  altitude. & Must & T/A & \todo{} \\
AV-013 & The rocket shall have a fast bidirectional communication link with
  GSE (separate from AV-012) that works to at least 100\,m. & Should & T & \todo{} \\
AV-014 & All electronic modules on the rocket shall be depowerable without
  opening the rocket. & Must & D & \todo{} \\
AV-015 & The rocket shall measure tank and chamber pressure (6 channels) at
  $\geq$\,15\,Hz per channel. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
AV-016 & Communication with GSE shall carry all required data using no more
  than 50\% of the allowed duty cycle. & Must & A/T & \todo{} \\
AV-017 & All radio communication shall support EU and USA settings, with an
  easy mode switch. & Must & I/D & \todo{} \\
AV-018 & All avionics components shall be modular and easy to replace under
  all operating conditions. & Must & I/D & \todo{} \\
AV-019 & Avionics shall be able to drive at least 8 servo motors. & Must & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-020 & Each servo-motor channel shall provide a way to disable motor power
  other than ARM. & Must & D/T & \todo{} \\
AV-021 & All external communication shall include a method of user
  authentication (cryptography). & Should & I/D & \todo{} \\
AV-022 & All boards shall measure internal board temperature and air
  temperature inside the rocket at $\geq$\,1\,Hz. & Must & T & \todo{} \\
AV-023 & The rocket shall measure acceleration, speed, air pressure, etc.\ at
  $\geq$\,15\,Hz. & Should & T & \todo{} \\
AV-024 & All components with factory cables shall use those cables; changing
  plugs on motors etc.\ is not allowed. & Must & I & \todo{} \\
AV-025 & All boards shall measure battery level and raise an alarm on
  anomalies. & Must & T/D & \todo{} \\
AV-026 & Igniter ports shall be immune to short circuit (one short-circuited
  primer shall not cause launch failure). & Must & T & \todo{} \\
AV-027 & Igniter ports shall detect short circuit and open circuit. & Should & T & \todo{} \\
AV-028 & All motors shall be commandable to the closed position from GSE. & Must & D/T & \todo{} \\
AV-029 & Only necessary actions shall be available from the GSE control
  panel (CP). & Must & I/D & \todo{} \\
AV-030 & All rocket components shall be remotely restartable from GSE. & Must & D/T & \todo{} \\
AV-031 & It shall be possible to achieve a rocket state safe to approach
  under all conditions. & Must & D & \todo{} \\
\end{reqtabular}

\subsection{Simulations}

\guidance{Simulations does not fly hardware, but owns analyses that
\emph{close} mission and vehicle requirements before flight / SF.}

\begin{reqtabular}
SIM-001 & A trajectory / performance model shall predict apogee for the
  baseline vehicle within \todo{tolerance} of MIS-001 before flight. & Must & A & \todo{} \\
SIM-002 & Stability / aero analysis shall confirm AERO-004 (static margin)
  for the as-built mass properties. & Must & A & \todo{} \\
SIM-003 & Structural / loads analysis shall support AERO-001 / AERO-002
  with documented assumptions. & Must & A & \todo{} \\
SIM-004 & Simulations shall deliver pre-flight / pre-SF prediction packages
  used in go/no-go (CONOPS). & Should & I & \todo{} \\
SIM-005 & \todo{CFD / thermal / other analysis requirements if needed.} & \todo{} & \todo{} & \todo{} \\
\end{reqtabular}
