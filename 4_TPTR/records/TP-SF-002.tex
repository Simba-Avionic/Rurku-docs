\subsection*{Plan}

\paragraph{Objective}
Retest after TP-SF-001 igniter retention failure; demonstrate ignition and
nominal burn per DRM-0400; collect chamber and manifold pressure, thrust, and tank telemetry;
close ENG-002 / ENG-003.

\paragraph{Configuration}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Propellants:} ethanol (fuel), nitrous oxide (oxidizer), nitrogen
    (pressurant).
  \item \textbf{Load cell:} 1$\times$ strain gauge, 240\,kHz, $\pm$1\,N.

\paragraph{Success criteria}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Successful ignition inside the combustion chamber.
  \item Chamber pressure and thrust within expected band for the planned burn
    duration (5\,s nominal).
  \item Shutdown sequence completes without critical hardware damage.
  \item Complete DAQ log and post-test photos.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Failure / abort criteria}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Misfire $\rightarrow$ hold; follow misfire wait and approach procedure.
  \item Loss of critical telemetry (chamber pressures, load cell) $\rightarrow$
    HOLD / abort.
  \item Sensor out of limits before ignition $\rightarrow$ HOLD / abort.
  \item Combustion outside the combustion chamber (e.g.\ ejected igniters).
  \item Hard start / overpressure $\rightarrow$ execute D-3 hot abort; safing per
    D-1 / D-2.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Report}

\paragraph{Outcome}
Pass (with anomalies) --- analysis dated 21.07.2026.

\paragraph{Results}
Ignition inside the chamber succeeded after the TP-SF-001 igniter remount.
Command timeline in checklist~C was executed as logged; main valves closed at
T$+$7\,s as planned. Visual burn from Dominik's GoPro~4 (\texttt{liquid static 2.mp4},
120\,fps) looked nominally stable for $\approx$1.3\,s after ignition.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Timeline vs.\ pressure + video} (ignition $t_0 \approx$ timestamp 504017\,ms
in \texttt{launch2.csv}; analysis window 503279--521043):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{$t \approx 0$:} Clear ignition on chamber pressure and on video.
  \item \textbf{$0$--$\approx$750\,ms:} Stable operation; chamber $\approx$14.5--16\,bar with a
    short spike near $t \approx 250$\,ms.
  \item \textbf{$\approx$750\,ms:} Chamber pressure begins to fall; ethanol injector /
    manifold pressure rises correspondingly. Not obvious on the video.
  \item \textbf{$\approx$1300\,ms:} Clear loss of thrust / chamber pressure on video and
    on the pressure trace.
  \item \textbf{$\approx$2400\,ms:} Sudden engine ``excitation'' / possible hard transient
    on film; ethanol manifold pressure collapses while chamber pressure stays nearly
    flat.
  \item \textbf{$\approx$2.5--7\,s:} Quasi-equilibrium with low-frequency (chugging) combustion
    instability.
  \item \textbf{$t = 7$\,s:} Planned shutdown; main valves close.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Anomalies}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No usable thrust-stand / load-cell recording despite successful calibration
    earlier the same day.
  \item Ethanol tank supercharge $\approx$40\,bar vs.\ 32.5\,bar planned.
  \item Large ethanol pressure loss on cooling jacket - $\approx$10\,bar.
  \item Chamber pressure decay from $\approx$750\,ms with concurrent ethanol manifold
    rise; visible thrust loss by $\approx$1.3\,s.
  \item Hard transient / possible energetic event at $\approx$2.4\,s (manifold pressure
    collapse; film shows sudden plume excitation).
  \item Low-frequency chugging-like instabilities from $\approx$2.5\,s to shutdown.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Pressurizing system issue}
Pressures inside run-tanks fall very rapidly as the engine ignites. 
They are expected to stay at their set pressures, which indicates an issue with the pressurizing system.
This issue turned out to be check-valves placed on both pressurizing lines (for Ethanol and Nitrous), which had 
a maximum flow capacity of only 20 liters per minute, whereas flow rate needed for nominal operation is closer to 
4 liters per second. Also, the technical Nitrogen bottles like the one used for pressurization, could have a 
flow restraining orifice inside their valve.
\paragraph{Cooling jacket pressure loss issue} 
Pressure readings show higher than calculated ($\approx$10\,bar vs $\approx$4.23\,bar) pressure loss on the engine's cooling jacket.
This is probably a result of higher than expected surface roughness inside the cooling channels. 
This must be accounted for when selecting supercharge pressure for the next static fire test.
\paragraph{Injector blow-up and combustion instabilities}
Most probably due to ethanol pressure being lower than expected for reasons discussed above, Ethanol injection manifold 
cracked and deformed at about 2400\,ms, and increased injection area caused visible energetic event and a 
very fuel rich and unstable combustion later on. This could be avoided by fixing issues with pressurization system and accounting for
increased cooling jacket pressure loss.

\paragraph{Follow-ups}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Manufacture new injector lower plate and pintle element
  \item Fix check valve issue - get rid of them or change to ones of higher flow capacity.
  \item Increase ethanol's supercharge pressure.
  \item Pressurize directly from COPV, not from the bottle.
  \item Fix thrust-stand logging before the next static fire.
\end{itemize}
